# CoMic Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

CoMic turns your Android phone into a microphone for your Windows PC. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the CoMic phone app and the CoMic program for Windows (together, "CoMic").

The short version: your voice goes from your phone straight to your own computer. We don't receive it, store it or listen to it. We don't have accounts, ads or analytics.

## What CoMic processes

**Your microphone audio.** When you tap Start, the phone app records sound from the microphone and sends it to the CoMic program on the computer you connect to, over your local Wi-Fi, a USB cable or Bluetooth. The audio is processed in real time (voice effects, volume, filters) and played into a virtual audio device on that computer. It is not saved to a file and it is not sent to us or to any server. When you tap Stop, recording stops.

**Connection details.** To find and connect to your computer, the apps exchange local network addresses (for example `192.168.1.10`), the computer's name and a pairing code. This stays inside your local network and is not sent to us.

**Your settings.** Your choices (language, voice, slider positions, port, startup options) are saved only on your phone and your computer.

**Purchases.** If you buy CoMic Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play. We receive from Google only what is needed to confirm that your purchase is valid (a purchase token and the product you bought). We never see your card or bank details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What CoMic does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No recordings are kept, by us or by the apps.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties, apart from Google Play processing your payment.

## Permissions on the phone

- **Microphone:** to capture your voice. Without it the app cannot work.
- **Network access:** to send audio to your computer on your local network.
- **Foreground service (microphone) and notifications:** so the microphone keeps working with the screen off, and so a visible notification always shows that the microphone is in use, with a Stop button.
- **Bluetooth** (only if you use the Bluetooth method): to connect to a paired computer.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android settings.

## Security

When "Require pairing code" is on, the audio between your phone and your computer is encrypted, so other devices on the same network cannot listen. If you turn it off, anyone on your local network who runs suitable software could in principle receive the audio stream. We recommend keeping it on, especially on public Wi-Fi.

## Legal basis

We process data only to provide the service you ask for (sending your microphone to your computer) and, for purchases, to fulfil our contract with you. This follows Moldovan Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection and, for users in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask for correction or deletion, object to processing, and complain to a data protection authority. In practice we hold no personal data about you apart from purchase confirmations from Google Play. Write to [EMAIL].

In Moldova, the authority is the National Center for Personal Data Protection (datepersonale.md). In the EU, you can contact the data protection authority of your country.

## Children

CoMic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly process data of children. If you believe a child has made a purchase, contact Google Play for a refund and write to us.
