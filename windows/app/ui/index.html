Get started

Three steps to use your phone as a microphone.

1Install CoMic on your phoneAndroid app. Keep phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi, or plug in a USB cable.
2Install VB-CableThe free virtual audio cable CoMic plays into. Restart Windows after installing.
3Pick “CABLE Output” in your appIn Discord, CS2 or Chrome, choose CABLE Output as the microphone.
